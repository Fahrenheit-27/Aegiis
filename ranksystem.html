Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Aegiis rank system and progression mechanics.

How is my rank progress calculated?

Your rank progress is primarily determined by the number of challenges you've successfully completed. Each completed challenge adds to your total count, with progress toward the next rank shown as a percentage.

Additional factors that can boost your progress include maintaining streaks, contributing to the community, completing diverse challenge types, and participating in special events.

Can my rank decrease over time?

No, once you've achieved a certain rank, it's permanently yours. You'll never lose a rank you've earned, even if you take a break from Aegiis.

However, weekly and monthly leaderboard positions can fluctuate based on your recent activity compared to other community members.

How do challenge difficulty levels affect my rank?

While all successfully completed challenges count toward your rank progression, higher difficulty challenges may offer additional benefits:

- Medium difficulty challenges count as 1 challenge toward your rank

- Hard difficulty challenges count as 1 challenge but may offer bonus achievement points

- Expert difficulty challenges count as 1 challenge but offer specialized recognition and faster streak progression

Are there any other benefits to higher ranks?

Yes, higher ranks unlock numerous platform benefits beyond those listed in the rank descriptions:

- Enhanced profile customization options

- Access to exclusive Discord channels

- Opportunities to contribute to challenge creation

- Priority access to platform support

- Eligibility for occasional real-world perks and recognition
